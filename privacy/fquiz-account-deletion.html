      Account deletion

      F Quiz

      How to permanently delete your F Quiz account and what happens to your data.

      Delete your account in the app

      Account deletion is available directly in F Quiz on iOS and Android.

      1. Open F Quiz and go to Profile.
      2. Sign in with your e-mail account if needed.
      3. Scroll to the Account section.
      4. Enter your current password in the account deletion field.
      5. Tap Delete Account.
      6. Review the warning and confirm deletion.
      Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

      Account data deleted

      When deletion completes, the account-linked records managed by F Quiz are deleted or released:

      • Firebase Authentication account and sign-in e-mail
      • Online profile, username, selected flag, points, statistics and synchronized mission progress
      • Presence record and reserved username

      Data outside the account

      • Data stored locally on your device may remain until you clear the app data or uninstall the app.
      • Aggregated or de-identified analytics and advertising records are handled according to the relevant provider settings and policies.
      • Limited records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations or backups.

      Processing time

      In-app deletion normally completes immediately after confirmation. If technical support is required, we aim to complete a verified request within 7 days.

      Cannot access the app?

      Contact CAREO LIMITED from the e-mail address registered to your F Quiz account and include your username if known. Never send your password.
