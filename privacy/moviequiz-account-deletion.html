      Delete your account

      Movie Quiz

      You can permanently delete your Movie Quiz account and its associated account data directly inside the app.

      Delete inside Movie Quiz

      1. Open Movie Quiz and go to Profile.
      2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
      3. Scroll to Delete Account. Email and password accounts must confirm the current password.
      4. Tap Delete Account and confirm the permanent deletion.
      Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your deleted account and account data cannot be recovered.

      Data deleted with your account

      • Firebase Authentication account
      • Movie Quiz profile and account-linked username
      • Account-linked points, missions and leaderboard profile
      • Account-linked multiplayer and presence data where technically identifiable

      What may remain

      Local app data stays on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the app. Aggregated, anonymized, security, fraud-prevention, advertising or analytics records may be retained where they are not reasonably linked to your account or where limited retention is required by law.
