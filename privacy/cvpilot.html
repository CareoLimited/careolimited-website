      Privacy Policy - CVPilot

      Last updated: October 2026
      App: CVPilot
      Developer / Publisher: CAREO LIMITED
      Contact: support@careolimited.com

      CVPilot respects your privacy. This Policy explains what information may be processed, how it is used, and your rights when you use the mobile application on iOS and Android.

      1. Data Processing Overview

      Most CVPilot data stays on your device. To provide CV analysis, job matching, cover letters, interview questions, and writing suggestions, the text you submit for these features is sent to the CVPilot AI service and processed by Anthropic’s Claude AI models, as described in Section 4. We do not sell personal data and we only use third-party services where they are needed for the features described below.

      2. Locally Stored Data

      The app may store resume content, job description text, analysis and match results, history, usage limits, app preferences, profile details, CV builder drafts, export counters, review prompt state, and premium status locally on your device. This data is used to provide app features, remember preferences, support offline use where available, and keep the app experience consistent. Some local records may include the CV or job description text you entered, imported, or saved as a draft. The app also stores a random app-generated device identifier, your choice about AI processing, and recent AI results so that re-running the same text returns the same result without sending it again.

      3. Files, Photos, Camera, Clipboard, and URL Imports

      • If you import a resume from Files, Photos, or Camera, the selected file or image is processed to extract resume text for app features.
      • Photo library and camera access are requested only when you choose to import or capture a resume image.
      • Clipboard access is used only when you choose to paste or copy CV, job description, or support contact text.
      • If you import a job description from a URL, the app connects to that website to retrieve readable text. The website may receive ordinary technical request information such as IP address, user agent, request time, and the URL requested under its own privacy policy.
      • File parsing, PDF text extraction, DOCX conversion, and image OCR may be incomplete or inaccurate, especially for scanned, blurred, protected, unusual, or poorly formatted documents.
      • When you export a CV as a PDF, the app may create a temporary file and use the device sharing or printing tools you select.

      4. AI Processing

      When you start a CV analysis, job match, cover letter, interview preparation, or an AI suggestion in the CV builder, CVPilot asks for your permission the first time and then sends the relevant text to its AI service. Nothing is sent before you allow it, and you can keep using the app’s other features if you decline.

      • Data sent: the CV text, job description text, or CV builder content you submit for that feature, the options you choose (such as tone, length, or seniority), your plan type (free or premium), and a random device identifier created by the app. Do not include information in these texts that you do not want processed.
      • Service providers: the CVPilot AI service runs on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.) and forwards each request to Anthropic, PBC, whose Claude models generate the result. Cloudflare and Anthropic act as service providers (processors) for these features.
      • Your text is used only to produce the result you requested. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, content sent through its API is not used to train its AI models. Anthropic may keep inputs and outputs for a limited period for safety and legal compliance, as described in its own privacy policy.
      • CAREO LIMITED does not store the content of your CV, job description, or builder text on its servers. The AI service records technical information only, such as request time, request type, error codes, and usage counts, and keeps a daily request counter per device identifier to prevent abuse.
      • Cloudflare may process technical request information, such as IP address and request time, to operate its network, under its own privacy policy.
      • AI features require an internet connection and are unavailable while you are offline.
      • Reporting AI content: if you use the Report button on AI-generated content, the reported text, the reason you chose, the app version, and your platform (iOS or Android) are sent to the CVPilot AI service and stored on Cloudflare so we can review the report. Your CV and the device identifier are not included. Reports are deleted automatically after 12 months.

      5. Processing Purposes

      • Provide resume analysis, job matching, CV builder, export, history, and settings features
      • Generate AI-based CV analysis, job match results, cover letters, interview questions, and writing suggestions
      • Remember user preferences and saved drafts
      • Apply free usage limits and premium access rules
      • Process purchases or premium access where applicable
      • Improve stability, prevent abuse, and keep the app functioning reliably
      • Review reports about AI-generated content and improve its safety and accuracy

      6. Third-Party and Platform Services

      The app may use platform or third-party services including Apple App Store, Google Play, Apple or Google in-app review services, device file/photo/camera/sharing/printing tools, and font rendering used for PDF export styling. These services operate under their own privacy policies. AI features use Cloudflare (hosting of the CVPilot AI service) and Anthropic (Claude AI models), as described in Section 4.

      7. Advertising and Tracking

      CVPilot does not display third-party ads and does not use third-party advertising tracking SDKs. Text you submit for AI features is used only to provide those features and is never used for advertising.

      8. Automated Results, Accuracy, and User Responsibility

      CVPilot provides automated, AI-generated CV analysis, job matching, scoring, keyword suggestions, cover letters, interview questions, and writing suggestions for informational purposes only. AI-generated content can contain mistakes or statements that are not true for you, and results may be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific employer, industry, applicant tracking system, or hiring process. Never add skills, experience, or results that you do not have. CVPilot does not guarantee interviews, job offers, hiring outcomes, CV acceptance, score accuracy, or compatibility with every recruitment system. You are responsible for reviewing all outputs before using them, verifying your CV and application materials, and making your own career, job application, hiring, legal, financial, or professional decisions.

      9. Best Performance and Supported Input

      For best results, use clear English CVs and English job descriptions with standard section headings, complete work history, skills, responsibilities, requirements, and measurable achievements. CVPilot may perform less accurately with non-English content, incomplete job posts, highly specialized or uncommon roles, unusual CV formatting, scanned or blurry files, images with poor text quality, or documents where text cannot be extracted reliably.

      10. Purchases and Premium Features

      Optional purchases, subscriptions, or premium access may be processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store full payment card details. Purchase status, subscription entitlement, renewal, cancellation, refund handling, and store account data may be managed by the relevant app store under its own terms and privacy policy. Purchase status may be stored locally or verified through the relevant store.

      11. Data Sharing

      We do not sell personal data. Text you submit for AI features is shared with Cloudflare and Anthropic only to produce the requested result, as described in Section 4. Other locally stored app content is not shared with CAREO LIMITED servers. Limited data may be processed by platform or third-party providers only as described in this Policy or when you choose to use device sharing, printing, store, review, or URL import features. The only exception is a report you choose to send about AI-generated content, which is stored as described in Section 4.

      12. Data Retention and Deletion

      Local data remains on your device until you delete saved entries where available, reset relevant app data where available, or uninstall the app. Temporary export files may be stored in the device cache. Store payment records and platform review or purchase records are retained by Apple or Google according to their own policies. Text sent for AI features is not kept on CAREO LIMITED servers after the result is returned; Anthropic may keep it for a limited period as described in Section 4. The daily request counter is used only to apply usage limits. Reports you send about AI-generated content are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted automatically.

      13. User Rights

      Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing. Because most app data is stored locally, you can usually manage or remove it directly through the app or your device settings. You may also contact us for privacy questions. CAREO LIMITED does not keep a copy of the text you send to AI features, so there is usually nothing on our side to access or delete; contact us if you have questions about this processing. Reports about AI-generated content are stored without your CV or device identifier, so we usually cannot link a report to you.

      14. Data Security

      We use HTTPS for external communications where applicable and design our apps to minimize data collection. No software or device environment can guarantee absolute security. The CVPilot AI service accepts requests only from the app over HTTPS and keeps the AI provider’s access key on the server, never in the app.

      15. Children’s Privacy

      The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

      16. International Data Transfers

      Platform and third-party providers may process limited data in countries outside your own. Text you submit for AI features is processed by Anthropic in the United States and passes through Cloudflare’s global network. Appropriate safeguards apply where required by law.

      18. Policy Updates

      We may update this Policy if app features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be reflected with a new Last updated date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the updated Policy.
