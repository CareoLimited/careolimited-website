      Account deletion

      Dishtopia

      How to permanently delete your Dishtopia account and what happens to your data.

      Delete your account in the game

      You can delete your Dishtopia account directly in the game on iOS and Android. Dishtopia accounts have no password, so none is needed.

      1. Open Dishtopia.
      2. On the Home screen, tap the gear button to open Settings.
      3. Tap Delete Account near the bottom of Settings.
      4. Read the warning and tap Delete to confirm.
      5. Stay connected to the internet until you see “Your account was deleted.”
      Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your kitchen, coins, items and progress are removed from the cloud and from that device.

      Data deleted

      When deletion completes, the data kept for your account in Unity Gaming Services is deleted:

      • Your Unity player account and player ID, including any link to Sign in with Apple or Google Play Games
      • Your cloud save: level, coins, items, kitchen progress and other game progress
      • Your place in your Kitchen Crew (the crew keeps the points it earned)
      • Your scores on the Chef's League leaderboard
      • Your gameplay analytics data (we ask Unity Analytics to delete it)
      • The game progress saved on that device, which is reset to a fresh start

      Data outside the account

      • If the leaderboard step cannot run (for example while offline), your generated player name and scores may stay visible on the Chef's League for a while. We remove them when you ask (see below).
      • Purchases stay with your Apple or Google account and can be restored with Restore Purchases. Payment records are kept by Apple or Google.
      • Advertising and consent records are handled by Google under its own settings and policies.
      • Limited records may be kept where needed for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

      Processing time

      Deletion in the game normally completes straight away. Requests sent by email are normally completed within 7 days once we have identified your account.

      Can't use the game, or already uninstalled it?

      Email CAREO LIMITED with the subject “Dishtopia Account Deletion”. Include your player name as shown in the game (for example “Sudsy Raccoon”), the Player ID shown at the bottom of Settings if you can still open the game (tap it to copy), your device (iPhone or Android) and, if you signed in, whether you used Sign in with Apple or Google Play Games. You can use the same email to ask us to remove leaderboard or crew entries. Never send passwords or payment details. We may ask a few questions to confirm the account is yours.
