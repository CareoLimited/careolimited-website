      TSI

      Tecto Structural Intelligence

      TSI reads photos of visible damage in structures together with engineering context, inspection history and technical knowledge. It reports what it sees, what that may mean and what to do next, and never treats a photo alone as proof that a structure is safe.

      How it reasons

      A careful reading, never a verdict

      TSI keeps three things apart in everything it produces, so an observation is never mistaken for an engineering conclusion.

      1. 1

        Observation

        What the evidence directly supports.

        A diagonal crack is visible on the beam surface, about 1.2 mm wide, subject to scale uncertainty.

      2. 2

        Interpretation

        What the pattern may fit, given the context.

        Its direction may fit a shear-related pattern, but a photo alone can't confirm the cause.

      3. 3

        Recommendation

        What to consider doing next.

        Have a qualified structural engineer inspect the beam, especially if the crack is new or growing.

          Who it's for

          Built for the people who look after buildings

          TSI is in research and development; these are the people it is being built for.

          • Homeowners and residents

            Photograph a crack or a damp patch and get a calm, plain explanation of what it may mean and when to call an engineer.

          • Building and site managers

            Keep a dated, comparable record of damage across buildings, and see what needs attention first.

          • Engineers and inspectors

            Start from structured observations with their sources, so site visits and reports begin a step ahead.
